About ExamPulse

A structured platform for test practice and performance analysis.

Our Purpose

ExamPulse is designed to provide candidates with a structured environment for attempting online tests and understanding their preparation.

Instead of focusing only on the final score, the platform will analyze accuracy, time, subject performance, topic performance and difficulty-level performance.

What ExamPulse Provides

  • Scheduled and daily tests
  • Online examination interface
  • Automatic scoring
  • Answer explanations
  • Test history
  • Performance analytics
  • Rule-based performance assessment
